    Turnkey service

    From choosing the tree to its first pruning on your site. One contact, no subcontractors to chase.

    1. 1

      Selection

      In person at Třebíz, or from photographs of specific pieces at growers in Japan, the Netherlands, Germany and Belgium.

    2. 2

      Transport of root-balled specimens, cranes, site access. Japanese growers taught us how to handle mature trees.

    3. 3

      Site preparation, substrate matched to the species, anchoring and watering. Each tree is handed over with a care plan.

    4. 4

      Shaping, health checks and winter protection, so that in ten years the tree looks better than on the day it was planted.

      Frequently asked questions

      Do you also sell smaller plants for an ordinary garden?

      Yes. The showroom stocks bamboo, Japanese maples, rhododendrons, azaleas, magnolias, perennials, substrates and fertilisers. Shaped trees range from smaller pieces for a home garden to large specimens.

      Will Japanese shaped trees survive a Czech winter?

      We import from growers in northern Japan, where winters are comparable to ours. With every tree we hand over a care plan, including winter protection for the first years.

      How much does a shaped tree cost?

      The price depends on the species, age, size and complexity of the shape. It ranges from smaller garden pieces to specimens costing hundreds of thousands of crowns. We will send you a specific offer with a photograph of the piece.

      Do you handle delivery and planting?

      Yes, including cranes and heavy machinery. See Turnkey service.

      Can I visit in winter or outside opening hours?

      Yes, by appointment. Call us or fill in the form and tick private visit.

      Do you shape the trees after planting?

      Yes. We offer regular shaping and health checks, as a one-off or as a year-round service.
